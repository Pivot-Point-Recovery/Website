Volunteer

Recovery Happens When People Show Up

You don't need a clinical background to change someone's life. You need to be willing to show up — consistently, without judgment.

Ways to Help

Where Volunteers Make a Difference

Every role below is filled by someone who decided that a few hours a month was worth it.

Peer Recovery Support

Walk alongside someone early in recovery. Certified training is provided — lived experience is welcome but not required.

Mentoring

Provide one-on-one guidance to participants working toward stability in housing, employment, or education.

Events & Outreach

Help staff community events, recovery gatherings, and awareness campaigns across Northern Virginia.

Reentry & Reintegration

Support justice-involved individuals during the critical first months after release.

Administrative Support

Behind-the-scenes help with scheduling, data, communications, and the logistics that keep programs running.

Fundraising

Help build the donor base and community partnerships that sustain free, local recovery services.
