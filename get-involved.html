Recovery Is a Community Effort

Northern Virginia doesn't need one more program. It needs a community that shows up. Here's how you can be part of that.

Ways to Help

Find Your Way In

Whether you have an hour a month, a professional skill, or the means to give — there's a place for you here.

Volunteer

Mentor someone in early recovery, support reentry, staff a community event, or help behind the scenes. Training is provided.

Become a Volunteer

Give

Your gift funds peer mentorship, recovery navigation, and reentry support — services offered at no cost to participants.

Partner With Us

Employers, treatment providers, courts, faith communities, and civic groups — recovery works best when the whole community participates.

Why It Matters

Every Role Here Changes an Outcome

Peer-led programs increase engagement, improve retention, and produce better long-term outcomes than clinical services alone. That model only works when community members participate.

✓

Volunteers make consistent, long-term support possible

✓

✓

✓

Advocates reduce the stigma that keeps people silent
