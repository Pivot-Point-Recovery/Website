Our Services

An Integrated Continuum of Care

From the moment someone asks for help to the day they stand on their own, Pivot Point provides consistent, coordinated care across every stage of recovery.

Peer-Led Recovery Programming

Recovery coaching, group support, and leadership pathways led by Certified Peer Recovery Specialists with lived experience. Peer support increases engagement, reduces isolation, and helps individuals remain connected to care during critical periods of change. Through mentoring and group programming, participants gain practical guidance from people who understand their journey firsthand.

Recovery Navigation & Case Management

Connecting individuals to housing, employment, education, and community resources. Recovery does not occur in isolation — our navigators address the full spectrum of needs that influence success, helping participants build the stability and support systems required for lasting change.

View Employment Resources

Reentry & Reintegration Support

Justice-involved individuals face particularly high risks during reentry. Without coordinated support, many return to the same conditions that contributed to prior substance use and legal involvement. Pivot Point intervenes at this critical moment with recovery-oriented reentry planning, peer mentorship, and ongoing support that promotes stability, accountability, and court compliance.

Family & Community Engagement

Addiction affects entire families and communities. We work to strengthen understanding, build recovery-friendly networks, and reduce stigma across Northern Virginia. When recovery is visible and supported, communities become safer, healthier, and more resilient for everyone.

Veteran Mentorship

By Veterans, For Veterans

Pivot Point Recovery is proud to offer a dedicated mentorship program for veterans navigating recovery. Our veteran mentors are veterans themselves — men and women who have served, who understand the unique challenges of military life, and who have walked the path of recovery firsthand.

Every Pivot Point veteran mentor has completed the All Rise Veteran Mentor Training Course, ensuring they are equipped with the specialized knowledge and skills to support fellow veterans through substance use recovery, reintegration, and rebuilding their lives.

Whether you are a veteran seeking support or a veteran interested in becoming a mentor, we invite you to connect with us.

Our Approach

The Power of Peer-Led Support

Peer-led recovery support is the cornerstone of Pivot Point's model. Individuals with lived experience bring credibility, empathy, and hope that are uniquely powerful in recovery settings.

This model strengthens accountability, builds recovery capital, and reinforces the belief that lasting recovery is achievable. Our peer-led approach complements clinical services by grounding care in real-world experience, fostering trust, and building a culture of mutual support and belonging.
