Your Impact

What Your Gift Does

$25

Covers transportation so someone can get to a recovery meeting, court date, or job interview.

$50

Funds a one-on-one session with a Certified Peer Recovery Specialist.

$100

Supplies a reentry starter kit — essentials for someone leaving incarceration.

$250

Sponsors a month of recovery navigation: housing, employment, and benefits support.

$500

Underwrites a community recovery event that reaches dozens of families.

$1,000

Trains and equips a new volunteer peer mentor from intake through certification.

Other Ways to Give

More Than One Way to Help

Give by Check

Make checks payable to Pivot Point Recovery, Inc. Contact us for the current mailing address and we'll send a receipt for your records.

Employer Matching

Many employers double charitable gifts. Check the matching box when you give, and submit your employer's form — we'll provide whatever documentation they need.

Monthly Giving

Recurring gifts are the most valuable kind. They let us commit to participants for the long haul instead of one grant cycle at a time.

Sponsor a Program

Businesses and civic groups can underwrite a specific program or event. Get in touch and we'll build something that fits.

Questions About Giving?

We're glad to talk through where your gift goes, matching programs, or sponsorship options.
