Our Mission

Building a compassionate, recovery-centered community

Pivot Point Recovery exists to provide recovery-oriented, community-based services for individuals impacted by substance use disorders, trauma, justice involvement, and related life disruptions — through an integrated model of clinical services, peer-led support, and reintegration assistance.

Learn Our Story

The Challenge

Northern Virginia needs local recovery infrastructure

Despite its prosperity, Northern Virginia faces a growing addiction and overdose crisis — with limited local resources to match.

Residents Leave for Treatment

Many residents are forced to leave the county for recovery, separating them from the connections that sustain healing.

Reentry Without Support

Justice-involved individuals return without stable housing, employment, or recovery networks — increasing relapse risk.

Recovery Works Locally

Peer-led programs increase engagement, retention, and outcomes. Local recovery makes entire communities stronger.

"People are more than their worst moments."

What We Provide

An Integrated Continuum of Care

From the moment someone asks for help to the day they stand on their own.

Peer-Led Recovery Programs

Recovery coaching, group support, and leadership pathways led by specialists with lived experience.

Recovery Navigation

Case management connecting individuals to housing, employment, education, and community resources.

Reentry & Reintegration

Targeted support for justice-involved individuals transitioning back with stability and accountability.

Family & Community

Building recovery-friendly networks and reducing stigma across Northern Virginia.

Why Local Matters

Recovery is most sustainable when people heal within their own communities

Staying local allows people to maintain family connections, continue employment, and build sober routines where they live.

✓

Continuity of care from treatment through long-term recovery

✓

✓

Reduced relapse risk through community-based support

✓

Normalized recovery that reduces stigma for everyone
